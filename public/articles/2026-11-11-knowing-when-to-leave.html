Knowing When to Leave

November 11, 2026

This one's a bit existential not so much related to all the low programming A.I. and tech stuff I've been writing about recently.

Every now and again I get this feeling like something is uninteresting and a bit of a drag. This can be with work, or with hobbies like games or even painting to some extent. If I do something long enough eventually I just have this feeling of disinterest and melancholy. It's very hard to explain. But I know when I have this feeling it's time to stop doing whatever I was doing before and move onto something else (hyper fixation type things like embedded systems or big picture things like jobs etc.)

My team will probably hear about this later today anyways but I am leaving my current job after 5 years early next month. I had that same feeling like I had before, sometimes you just know. There's been way to many red flags as well especially more recently that make it feel like it's an extremely unhealthly place to work. I felt this a bit before I went on my vacation but right before I was going to come back I had that disinterest and melancholic feeling that I mentioned earlier. It was the day before I came back I was just feeling like, oh. I have to go back to work. Instead of being excited about talking to my coworkers and picking up where I left off it was none of that.

So I guess my advice to whoever's reading this is, look at what you're currently doing with your life. If you don't enjoy something then nothing will change unless you change it. Either by making your situation better or by stepping away.
