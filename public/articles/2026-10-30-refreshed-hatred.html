Refreshed Hatred

October 30, 2026

I went to the south of France for the past week or two on vacation with my girlfriend. Overall it was a very nice trip but with lots and lots of disruptions as you may have seen on the news. I was in Nice when they were rioting right across from the main street where looters smashed windows and beat and kicked each other. Rioting is obviously not the goal of a protest, but I genuinely understand how we got here.

The past few years have completely wrecked our societal fabric. You had covid, then the war in Ukraine, then AI came around, more wars, more rumours of wars, more job disruption, more hate. People are tired and people are angry. This protest turned riot in France was just a side effect of these inevitable outcomes from our current trajectory. I do feel in my gut that AI will in some way trigger a catastrophic outcome that we cannot recover from.

For me the biggest problem that I have with AI is it is built on theft and has become a very simple conduit for creative theft. Take this blog for example. If you wanted you could point a malicious AI at it say copy all the content and push it up to a new domain with slightly differents words, and in 99% of the models today they would just do it. No second thoughts they would just outright steal content and reproduce it as your own. That's how they were built though. AI was trained on our collective knowledge then sold back to us at a premium. This is so unbelievably wrong and makes me so incredibly angry but I'm in a position where I feel like I can't really do anything to resolve that. I have to use AI at my job or else I will be terminated for not being as fast as the others. I have to have 10x output compared to previous quarters with AI or I will be terminated. It is so enfuriating I am literally about to throw my laptop just typing this all out.

So what do we do? I honestly don't know. I just know I am going back to my job monday with zero interest and burn out. I am so sick of everything in this industry. It's all toxic from the top down and it doesn't have a clear positive path forward.

The only thing that has given me remotely any hope or joy is the goatmire conference where people just got together and talked about different things and shared all kinds of interesting hardware ideas. There's also the nerves EU meetup where people present interesting things too. All in all I think hardware may be my only path forward as it's not completely wiped out by AI. But again, we'll see.
